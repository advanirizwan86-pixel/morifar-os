# Features

## CRM

Manages clients, companies and pipeline context. Depends on `clients`, `companies`, `contacts` and `leads`. Demo data should be reset before live use.

## Leads

Captures and tracks incoming opportunities. Production-ready for manual lead entry; external marketing sync is future work.

## Tasks

Tracks daily operational work. Production-ready for internal task management; recurring tasks and calendar sync are future work.

## Company Formation

Tracks formation cases, milestones and required documents. Ready for internal tracking; government API submission is future work.

## Documents

Tracks document records and requests. Real file storage/upload remains future work.

## Approvals

Keeps sensitive actions under human control. Ready for daily use.

## Department Queues

Shows work by department. Ready after demo reset; queue automation rules are future work.

## AI Professionals

Directory of AI roles, workload and capabilities. Live autonomous execution is future work.

## AI Command Center

Executive oversight for AI workforce. Internal review-ready.

## Workflow Engine

Workflow templates, visual definitions and runs. Ready for design and monitoring; durable workflow runtime is future work.

## Executive Copilot

Leadership decision support. AI guidance must be reviewed by humans.

## Client Intelligence

Client health and next actions. Requires calibration after real data is collected.

## Operations Intelligence

Workload, risk and performance view. Ready for leadership review.

## Knowledge Base

Static SOP and reference library. Semantic search is future work.

## Service Automation

AI-assisted service workspace. Pilot-ready with human review.

## System Status

Shows version, release state, route health and risks. Ready.

## Settings

Company settings, Company Profile, departments, roles and markets. Ready.

## Search

Searches operational records. Semantic search is future work.

## Notifications

Shows internal notifications. Email/push delivery is future work.
